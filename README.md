# Why/My use case
2025, >7 years on, post viral ME/CFS has stolen my hands & leg functions & at times my voice, thus my ability to type is gone. I use this in conjunction w text messaging on PC & google translate readout for daily life communication & to survive. My hands are not completely paralyzed like they were in 2023 so I can do it w a controller & vtt.

(moderate-severe ME/CFS; if I was severe like in the past I wouldn't be able to focus to do this & I would be sleeping for months on end)

I hope it helps someone.
